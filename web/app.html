Connect to your computer

On the computer, switch OpenPad on and open its QR code (bar icon, or Pair in the OpenPad window). Then scan it here.

Couldn't reach the computer. Check that it's on the same Wi-Fi with OpenPad switched on. The first time, this iPad also needs OpenPad's certificate:

  1. Open the setup page and download the certificate.
  2. Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install.
  3. Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings › turn on OpenPad.
