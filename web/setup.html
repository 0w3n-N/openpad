Install OpenPad on this iPad

The installed app keeps your notebooks on the iPad, works with no Wi-Fi, and syncs to your computer when you're back on this network.

1 Trust your computer's certificate

The app talks to your computer over an encrypted connection. Your computer made its own certificate for that. It can only vouch for devices on private networks (addresses like 192.168.x.x), not for websites.

Download the certificate
  1. Tap Allow, then Close.
  2. Open Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install (enter your passcode).
  3. Open Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings, and turn on OpenPad.

2 Get the app

Already installed? Skip to step 3. Otherwise:

Open the OpenPad app

Then tap Share › Add to Home Screen › Add.

3 Connect it

Open OpenPad from your Home Screen, tap Connect to computer, and scan the QR code shown on your computer (bar icon, or Pair in the OpenPad window).

No camera? Under "Enter the address and PIN instead", type:

AddressPIN

The PIN changes every 10 minutes. This page keeps it current.

Rather not install anything? You can keep using OpenPad in Safari from the computer's QR code. It just needs the computer to be on.
